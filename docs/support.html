Updates

Shorthand updates itself. When a new version is out, a window shows what’s new with Install Update, Remind Me Later, and Skip This Version. Shorthand restarts, and your snippets, settings, permissions, and encryption carry over.

  • Check now: Shorthand ▸ Check for Updates…, or the same item in the menu bar menu.
  • Choose how: Settings ▸ General ▸ Updates, with Check for updates automatically (on by default, once a day) and Download and install updates automatically.
  • When an update is found in the background, Shorthand shows a gentle reminder instead of interrupting you. The update window comes to the front when you open it.
  • Safety: every update must carry the developer’s update signature and be signed by the same Apple Developer ID before it installs. The update check sends nothing about you or your snippets.

Downloads, release notes, and this help are also online at shorthandexpander.com. Questions: support@shorthandexpander.com.

Uninstalling Shorthand

Open Settings ▸ General and click Uninstall Shorthand…. If Shorthand is locked, unlock it first. Choose one:

  • Uninstall, Keep My Snippets: Shorthand quits, moves itself to the Trash, and removes its login item and Accessibility permission. Your snippets, backups and keys stay, so reinstalling later picks up where you left off.
  • Uninstall and Delete Everything…: also deletes your snippet library, every backup, your settings and statistics, saved AI keys and encryption keys. Shorthand asks a second time, because this can’t be undone. To keep a copy, export your groups first (right-click a group ▸ Export Group…).

A library in a sync folder (Dropbox, iCloud Drive) is never deleted, because your other Macs may use it.
