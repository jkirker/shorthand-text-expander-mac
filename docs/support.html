Support Shorthand

Shorthand is an independent app, made by one person. It isn’t affiliated with any other text-expander company; it simply imports from TextExpander so you can bring your snippets along.

There’s no subscription, no account and no ads. If Shorthand saves you time, you can buy me a coffee on Ko-fi. It’s entirely optional, and every feature works the same either way.

Button not showing? Use this link instead: Buy me a coffee on Ko-fi (ko-fi.com/kirkerplugins). It opens in your web browser.

You’ll also find Buy Me a Coffee on Ko-fi… in the Help menu and in the Shorthand menu bar icon’s menu, and a Ko-fi button in Settings ▸ General ▸ Help & Support.

Updates

Shorthand updates itself. When a new version is out, a window shows what’s new with Install Update, Remind Me Later and Skip This Version. Shorthand restarts, and your snippets, settings, permissions and encryption carry over.

  • Check now: Shorthand ▸ Check for Updates…, or the same item in the menu bar menu.
  • Choose how: Settings ▸ General ▸ Updates, with Check for updates automatically and Download and install updates automatically.
  • Safety: every update must carry the developer’s update signature and be signed by the same Apple Developer ID before it installs. The update check sends nothing about you or your snippets.

Downloads, release notes and this help are also online at github.com/jkirker/shorthand-releases and jkirker.github.io/shorthand-releases.
