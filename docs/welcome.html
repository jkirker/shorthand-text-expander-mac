Welcome to Shorthand

Type a short abbreviation, get your full snippet. Shorthand is a native Mac text expander that works in almost any app, and imports from TextExpander so your existing snippets come with you.

What Shorthand can do

  • Expand abbreviations as you type, with plain or formatted text: Abbreviations & Expansion.
  • Fill-ins, dates, sums and conditions: Fill-ins, Dates & Times, Calculations & Conditions.
  • Script snippets in AppleScript, shell or JavaScript: Script Snippets.
  • AI, if you want it: rewrite selected text, let a snippet write part of itself, or have AI suggest a snippet: AI Features.
  • Suggestions and reminders based on what you type often: Suggestions & Reminders.
  • Tags, multi-select and a Trash: label snippets across groups, move or tag many at once, and put back anything you delete: Tags, Trash & Multi-Select.
  • A blocklist: keep Shorthand out of password managers, banking sites and any app or website you choose: Privacy, Blocklist & Permissions.
  • Per-app snippets: the same abbreviation can type different text in different apps: Per-App Snippets.
  • Search and hotkeys to insert any snippet without remembering its abbreviation: Search, Menus & Hotkeys.
  • Encryption and an app lock: encrypt your library, settings and backups, and unlock Shorthand with Touch ID: Security & Encryption.
  • Sync and backups through Dropbox, iCloud Drive or similar: Sync, Backups & Restore.

Finding your way

  • Shorthand lives in the menu bar. Click its icon for search, your snippet groups, and settings.
  • The main window (Open Shorthand in the menu) is where you edit snippets.
  • Type in the search field at the top of this help window’s sidebar to search every help page.
